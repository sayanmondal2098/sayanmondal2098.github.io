Smart Street Light Control System Using an Algorithm and Image Processing

A camera-based lighting-control concept that responds to road activity and vehicle density.

Research question

Adapt street illumination to observed road demand.

The preprint explores how road-camera observations can inform street-light operation. The goal is to reduce unnecessary illumination while retaining useful visibility as road activity changes.

Approach

From observation to a useful representation.

Camera images supply observations of road workload or vehicle density. Image processing and a control algorithm use that activity information to adjust the lighting configuration. The contribution is a proposed adaptive control concept.

Evidence and scope

Reading the contribution in context.

Publication evidence

ResearchGate dates the preprint to December 2017; the author uploaded the displayed full text in July 2020. Those dates describe different events. The public record provides a DOI and manuscript, but does not establish a peer-reviewed venue.

Interpretation

The intended benefits are reduced energy use and continued road visibility. This page does not claim a measured energy-saving percentage or a validated improvement in road safety. Weather, camera visibility, and control response would matter in deployment.
