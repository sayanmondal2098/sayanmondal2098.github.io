Some Observations and Problems Explored on Dimensionality Reduction of Text Documents

An exploratory technical report on difficulties encountered when reducing the dimensions of document embeddings.

Research question

Keep useful document structure in fewer dimensions.

Document embeddings can contain more dimensions than an analysis needs. This exploratory report examines difficulties arising during dimensionality reduction and discusses possible remedies before a broader research proposal.

Approach

From observation to a useful representation.

The public abstract describes an investigation of problems encountered while reducing document representations. It does not identify a verified algorithm configuration or benchmark. This page therefore keeps the method description at the level supported by that abstract.

Useful questions for this research area include whether nearby documents remain related after projection and whether downstream analysis retains meaningful structure. These are general interpretation questions, rather than experimental findings attributed to the report.
