My profile My library Metrics Alerts Settings Sign in View article Sign in Sayan Mondal ASTriA: Adaptive Semi-Supervised Tri-Learning Approach for Scalable Malicious URL Detection Authors Avijit Gayen, Sayan Mondal, Bhanu Bariar, Khokan Mondal, Angshuman Jana Publication date 2026/6/29 Conference 2026 IEEE Region 10 Symposium (TENSYMP) Pages 1-5 Publisher IEEE Description As entrance sites for phishing, malware transmission, and data theft, malicious URLs represent a serious cybersecurity risk. While supervised machine learning techniques require huge labeled datasets that are costly and time-consuming to gather, traditional blacklist-based protections are ineffective against newly generated URLs. Unsupervised methods, on the other hand, frequently misclassify harmless URLs and lack trustworthy advice. We suggest a self-training-based, lightweight, semi-supervised detection approach to get over these restrictions. Our proposed methodology expands the training set by iteratively assigning high-confidence pseudo-labels to a huge pool of unlabeled data and a limited collection of labeled URLs. To balance accuracy and efficiency, we used interpretable classifiers like Random Forest and k-Nearest Neighbors with lightweight lexical and host-based data.
